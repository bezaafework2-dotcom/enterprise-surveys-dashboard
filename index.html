Enterprise Surveys country dashboard

Pick a country, a topic and what to compare with. Each panel shows the country's latest survey, its previous survey, and the comparators you choose. Data: World Bank Enterprise Surveys, through the World Bank Data360 API.

Groups

Group figures are simple averages (or medians) of each country's most recent survey. They are calculated on this page and are not official World Bank aggregates. Countries are surveyed in different years, so comparisons mix survey years. "What stands out" ranks indicators by how far the country sits from the rest of its region and by how much it changed since its previous survey. This is a personal project and not an official World Bank product. Code and method: GitHub repository.
